## 12. Top 10 takeaways for a systematic strategy
1. Core trigger: a candle close across the 200 SMA on the execution timeframe (1m/5m/15m; 1h for swings). Long above, short below, with the stop on the other side of the 200 and beyond the nearest swing low or high.
2. Confirmation stack: price above the 200 SMMA ("smooth") as well; stochastic RSI low and turning for longs; 21 MA close to the 200 ("tight MAs").
3. Higher-timeframe filter: trade with the direction of the next-higher timeframes' 200 MAs. Use the cascade (1m, 5m/7m, 15m, 30m/45m, 1h, 2h, 4h, 8h, 12h, daily) both as a bias filter and as a sequence of targets.
4. Regime filter: daily 200 SMA closes (bull or bear swing bias), 3-day 28 MA (bull market intact), 5-day 200 MA (bear market). Hidden bullish divergence on 4h or higher plus low stochastics adds a long bias.
5. Entry refinement: prefer the retest of a broken flag, trendline or level, or the pullback to the 21 MA, over chasing breakout candles.
6. Targets: flag pole measured move, capped by VRVP POC or gaps and the next higher-timeframe 200. Require at least 3:1 R:R or skip.
7. Management: take about 25% at the halfway point to target and move the stop to break even. Take more profit when price stretches far from the 21 MA or the stochastics are overbought. Re-enter on the next 21-MA pullback flag.
8. Time filters: favor Monday night through Wednesday; skip or shrink size on Fridays, weekends, the last days of December, and the hours before CPI, PPI or FOMC.
9. Risk: fixed small dollar risk (about 0.5-1% of account), size reduced after rule deviations, cap at about 3 trades per day after reaching the day's profit (risk only house money), and keep stops far from liquidation. Backtest before adopting, because his 68/90/99% claims are not reliable.
10. Separate books: a long-term BTC spot stack (DCA when the 2-week stochastic RSI is low, i.e. in fear) apart from the trading account. Treat altcoins as optional, only when BTC.D and USDT.D are falling and the daily alt chart shows tight MAs, low stochastics and price above the POC.
