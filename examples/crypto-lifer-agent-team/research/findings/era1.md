## 12. Top 10 takeaways for a systematic strategy (this era)
1. **Core long setup:** a bullish pattern on the 1h/4h; enter at the lower trendline in the last 25% of the pattern; stop just below the pattern or last wick; target = measured move. Coding it needs a pattern detector, e.g. a regression-channel flag or wedge, so it can only be reproduced approximately.
2. **Momentum filter:** MACD histogram negative but rising ("pink"), with the MACD line turning up below zero, plus stochastics low on the 4h and the daily not overbought.
3. **Trigger refinement:** regular bullish RSI divergence on a lower timeframe (15m/1m), entering on the second RSI low, not the first.
4. **Alternative entry:** the breakout-and-retest of the upper trendline (a wick touch), not the breakout candle itself.
5. **Regime filter (the most codeable rule):** Trend Watch, i.e. close above or below the 144 WMA on the 4h/daily (long above, flat or short below). Add price relative to the 200 MA and the 50/200 cross.
6. **Confluence bonus:** the 200 MA crossing the lower part of the pattern or lying between entry and stop; Fib 0.5–0.618 zones.
7. **Sizing:** 1% portfolio risk per trade, size = risk / stop distance; require at least 2:1 (ideally 3:1) to the measured-move target, and set the take-profit slightly below the level.
8. **Trade management:** move to break-even at about +5–6%; scale in thirds; trail the stop along the 21, then 50, then 100 MA.
9. **Take-profit overlays:** price far above the daily 21 MA, bearish RSI divergence, or daily stochastics overbought. Expect mean reversion to the 21.
10. **Backtest everything before trusting it:** he never showed systematic results, his live record mixes clear hits (20k, 29k, the Nov 2020 pullback) with misses (75k, the Apr 13 long, 14k), and he broke his own stop rules. Drop the high leverage (20–34x), the no-stop accumulation, and news or event trades.
