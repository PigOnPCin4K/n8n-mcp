## 2. Entry setups / triggers

- Price must be above the 200 MA.

## 12. Top 10 takeaways for a systematic strategy
1. **Regime filter:** trade long only when price is above the daily 200 SMA and short only below it. Add the 3-day 200 as a stricter macro gate (reduce size or skip longs below it).
2. **Core entry:** price above the 200 SMA on the trading timeframe, then a pullback or sideways move until it touches the 21 SMA. Optional filters: stochastic RSI turning up from below about 20, and the 21 above the 200. Test this on 15m/1h/4h; the 1m is too noisy.
3. **Mirror rule for shorts:** below the 200 with the 21 touching from below.
4. **Stop:** below the trading timeframe's 200 SMA or the pattern low, whichever is further. Size off that distance at 1% of equity.
5. **Targets:** require at least 2.5–3R. TP = measured move (about 70% of the prior impulse) or the next resistance or POC.
6. **Exit on extension:** a large distance between price and the 21 SMA (for example, more than N×ATR, to be tuned) means take profit or trail. Test whether moving to break-even after +1R helps or hurts.
7. **200-MA ladder as a target model:** a lost 200 on timeframe T projects to the 200 on the next higher timeframe (1h→2h, 6h→8h, D→2D→3D). This is testable and was right in Nov 2025.
8. **Daily/3-day tweezer reversal:** enter after the close (8pm ET / 00:00 UTC) with the stop beyond the wicks. Expect a low win rate and high R; backtest it on its own.
9. **Calendar filters:** smaller size and wider stops on weekends and holidays; no new risk before FOMC and until a few hours after; avoid Sunday-night and Friday-afternoon entries.
10. **Treat as unverified:** Bookmap liquidation magnets, Fib golden pocket, H&S/cup-and-handle reads and his stated hit rates. Add them only as optional confluence after the MA-based core has been backtested on its own. Never use the DCA bot or averaging down.
