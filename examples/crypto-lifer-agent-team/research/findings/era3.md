## 2. Entry setups / triggers
1. **Retest of a broken pattern or level.** He uses this setup most and calls it the safest. It works on any timeframe, usually 7m/15m/1h/4h, and in both directions. Price breaks out of a pattern, comes back to the broken line, and fails to re-enter ("when the price tries to get back inside a pattern but can't" [EKEQ7YqHCBg @ 0:13:56]). He enters on the retest, not the breakout: "retests are the safest trades to make in your life" [6MjK8znsZHI @ 0:59:58]. The retest of old resistance as new support is the long version [lG6eh5bbn6c @ 0:28:40]. The bearish retest of a lost POC or trendline is the short version [ZvgFVv8kD8g @ 1:18:38; SvNw7pJz8rc @ 0:24:29]. Oscillator condition: "basically 100% of the time a re-test occurs when the stokes are up" [iaYrEoXUYOw @ 1:56:23], so a bearish retest should come with high stoch.
2. **Bottom of channel/pattern on a stoch alert (long).** He draws the pattern, sets a stoch RSI alert, and buys only when the stoch is low at the lower trendline: "my whole trading style is based off setting alerts and either getting it at the bottom of the channel or the retest" [X4SxwT8XOY0 @ 0:18:20]. Also "I personally only buy the bottom of the channel" [Frn92GGYgkA @ 1:36:47] and "get in at the tightness of a wedge or at the bottom of the trend line" [NZLvEnQh45w @ 0:11:05]. He sets the alerts at the middle and bottom of the 1h stoch [ZrFcNX9zMQo @ 1:43:58].
3. **4h 200 SMA cross.** One of his stated "top 3 strategies." It runs on the 4h and goes both ways: "the second the four hour candle closed above this yellow line… open up a long… stop loss below the 200 SMA… short the same way… constantly bringing the stop loss to break even and you Trail it" [HY0eaj2A9qo @ 1:14:48]. He applied it live: a long at about 21.7k on the 4h close above the 200 SMA, target 29.4k [Frn92GGYgkA @ 1:52:56], and a short on the 4h close below the 200 SMA [c4U2qFbzSLQ @ 0:16:28].
4. **Tight MAs leading to a move to the 200 SMA.** When the 21/50/200 converge and price clears prior resistance or an HVN, he buys with the stop below the next support. The target is the 200 SMA: "when you get tight moving averages you Moon up to the 200 SMA… start buying in with a stop loss below the next resistance" [IQkQ3LEi99g @ 0:15:47; 7030jJvQdZI @ 0:05:21]. 6. **Pattern breakouts with measured moves.** Target = pole length. 7. **Divergence reversal.** Bullish or hidden-bullish RSI divergence, plus oversold on several timeframes, plus a support retest, is "the trifecta for me" [kn0MHdodwkg @ 1:43:54]. Bearish divergence with high stoch for shorts [joM97Vj8JT4 @ 0:10:08].

## 12. Top 10 takeaways for a systematic strategy
1. **Regime filter:** 4h close above or below the 200 SMA sets the bias (long-only above, short-only below). On the daily, add the 200 SMA reclaim and the total-market-cap weekly 200 SMA.
2. **Entry = retest, not breakout:** after a level or pattern break, enter on the first pullback to the broken line, with stoch RSI turning in the trade direction.
3. **Stoch RSI alignment** (defaults 3/3/14/14): the entry timeframe oversold and curling up, the next timeframe up not overbought, and no trade against the daily. Map: 1h trade uses 15m entries, 4h uses 1h, and so on.
4. **Stops** go beyond structure: below the 200 SMA, the POC, or 1–2 swing lows. Typically about 10% on alts. Set the target at 3R or more (10/30).
5. **Targets** come from VPVR: the next HVN or POC, the measured move, or the 200 SMA when MAs are tight. Take 50–70% at TP1, then move the stop to break-even and trail.
6. **Momentum-exhaustion exit:** price far above the 21 MA, stoch overbought, and RSI above 70 means take profit or expect a flag.
7. **MA compression** (21/50/200 tight) plus a reclaim of an HVN is a setup with a 200 SMA magnet target.
8. **POC rule:** a lost POC plus a failed reclaim means a short toward the next HVN. A reclaimed POC is long-bias.
9. **Time filters:** no new entries from Sunday night to Monday morning or during Fed/CPI windows. Prefer Tue–Thu.
10. **Altcoins:** trade them only when BTC is flat or up and total market cap holds key zones. Choose liquid coins showing relative strength. Avoid new listings. Test the "68%" pattern odds yourself rather than assuming them.
