## 12. Top 10 takeaways for a systematic strategy
1. **Core rule**: long on a candle *close* above the 200 SMA, short on a close below. The stop sits beyond the 200 (or the nearest POC/swing). This is testable on 1m/5m/15m/1h/4h.
2. **Regime filter**: only take trigger-TF longs when the next higher TF (e.g. 4h/8h) is above its 200 SMA, and shorts when below. He leaves this filter implicit; making it explicit fixes his counter-trend losses.
3. **Take-profit ladder**: next higher-TF 200 SMA, then measured move, then POC/VAH or the volume-profile gap. Exit early when price is stretched from the 21 SMA or Stoch RSI is overbought.
4. **Add-on/re-entry**: after a 200 break, the first pullback to the 21 SMA that does not close back through the 200.
5. **Setup quality filter**: compressed SMA band (21/50/100/200 within X% of each other), Stoch RSI below 20 and turning up, RSI below 50, MACD histogram rising. Treat it as a confluence score and test which terms add edge.
6. **Minimum 3:1 planned R:R**, with a quick move to break-even. Measure whether break-even-at-+1R helps or hurts expectancy; his "3 of 10 wins" maths assumes full targets, which he admits he rarely holds to.
7. **Chop guard**: stand aside after 2 consecutive stop-outs on the same trigger TF in a session. Optionally skip weekend nights, Fed/CPI windows and holiday weeks, and test each as a filter.
8. **HTF bias from weekly closes** relative to marked S/R levels and the weekly/daily 200 SMA. Use it to size or allow direction, not to time entries; his HTF price targets missed often.
9. **Fixed fractional risk** (e.g. 0.5–1% of equity per trade) in place of his fixed-dollar amount at extreme leverage. Cap leverage so liquidation sits at least 2x the stop distance away.
10. **Spot DCA module**: accumulate on fear (extreme lows in the Fear & Greed index), holding equal reserve capital for a second tranche, and trim into big green candles far from the 21. Keep it separate from the leverage scalping book.
