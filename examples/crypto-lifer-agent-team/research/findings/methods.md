# Crypto Lifer (Sam): what the "method" streams actually teach

Sources: tutorials pack plus the full transcripts of all 9 streams. Timestamps are the start of the ~60s caption line that contains the quote. He says "21-day moving average" on every timeframe; he means the 21-period SMA of whatever chart is open. His toolkit: SMA 21/50/100/200 (SMA only, "I don't use EMAs" [Kb0ZHTIp4Cc @ 1:27:56]), Stochastic RSI, RSI, MACD, chart patterns with measured moves, horizontal S/R, Bookmap liquidity/liquidations, and sometimes VRVP point of control.

## 1. "My short strategy revealed" — uAAUw-tQ8us (2022-02-03)

**Promise:** his short strategy.
**What he teaches:** one clear rule, followed by a timeframe-alignment filter.
1. On the 4h, mark the previous swing high.
2. If the Stoch RSI cycles back up to overbought and price fails to make a new high above that previous high, short.
3. Don't short while the 4h is oversold. Prefer to wait until the 4h "rolls" and prints a lower high, so the daily and the 4h both point down.
4. If timeframes disagree (for example 1h overbought and 4h oversold), don't trade. Drop to the 5m, draw the flag, and take the direction it breaks.
5. Have a plan ready for both directions: an alert level, a long trigger and a short trigger.

- "you were over bought on the stochastics rsi and you couldn't make a previous high dumpage … that's really a strategy you can use for the rest of your life in any single market if it's stochastic comes back to the upside and does not break the previous high short it" [uAAUw-tQ8us @ 0:19:04–0:20:04]
- "four hour is oversold i do not decide to short when the four hours oversold" [@ 0:35:12]
- "what i'm waiting for is the four hour to roll make a lower high then i'm gonna short that puppy … then you're gonna have the daily in your favor the four hour in your favor" [@ 0:43:18]
- "if the one hour can retrace if we don't make a lower low here than thirty six thousand two hundred then i would long that four hour" [@ 0:44:18]
- "i do not like to long when the one hour is high i like to short that however i'd be fighting the four hour … when that's the case i form flags … the flag will tell me on the smaller time frame which direction" [@ 0:45:18]

He gives no stop, no target beyond pattern measured moves, and no size. **Verdict:** the core rule (Stoch RSI overbought + failure to make a higher high = short) can be repeated and coded. Everything else is discretionary.

## 2. "This one indicator tells all" — Kb0ZHTIp4Cc (2023-11-14)

Exit when the 7m gets overbought: "I trade stochastics stochastics got overbought on the seven told you that the ride was over" [@ 1:34:25].

6. **Exit:** "sell green candles over the bottom of the one hour and the 15 minute … take profit at resistance … sell it resistance buy into support" [@ 1:15:10]. Treat Monday moves as suspect ("false move Monday").

**Verdict:** a set of rules of thumb. Items 2, 4 and 5 can be coded. "Big gains" is never defined.

## Consolidated mechanical rules

Notation: SR = Stochastic RSI (%K); OB = overbought (SR > 80); OS = oversold (SR < 20). These thresholds are my defaults; he only says "high", "low" and "dotted line". SMAn(tf) = n-period SMA on that timeframe.

**A. Regime and bias**
1. IF the 1h candle closes below SMA200(1h) (he treats 1 close as a warning, ≥2 as confirmed) THEN intraday bias = bearish, close longs, and target = SMA200(4h) (secondary: SMA200(2h)).
2. IF the 1h candle closes above SMA200(1h) THEN intraday bias = bullish.
3. IF SMA21(4h) > SMA200(4h) THEN swing regime = long-only setups (his 2026 "same setup every time").
4. IF the weekly close is below a key horizontal level / weekly support THEN macro bias = bearish until a weekly close reclaims it. A weekly bullish engulfing close above the prior two closes = cautiously bullish.
5. IF the 4h prints a lower high AND a lower low below the prior swing low THEN downtrend is confirmed (short setups allowed). A higher high cancels it.
6. Macro bearish escalation only IF the weekly trendline is lost AND the 4h closes below SMA200(4h).

**B. Vetoes**
7. Never open a long when SR(entry tf) is OB, or when SR(1h) and SR(4h) are both OB.
8. Never open a short when SR(4h) is OS. Stop adding shorts when the 4h RSI, SR and MACD are all at extremes.
9. IF the 1h and 4h SR point opposite ways ("indecision") THEN no trade. Drop to the 5m and trade only the break of the 5m flag.
10. Reduce size or skip on Mondays ("false move Monday"), CPI/FOMC days and Sunday nights. Skip after a structure break ("good day not to trade").

**C. Long entries**
11. **200 bounce:** bias bullish (rule 2) AND price touches SMA200(1h) AND holds (aggressive: at the touch; conservative: after sideways consolidation above it). Stop: below the SMA200 / the next swing low. TP: next resistance, with R:R ≥ 2:1 (preferably 3:1 or better).
12. **Compression breakout:** SMA21(4h) > SMA200(4h) AND the 21/50 cluster tightly near price (spread < x% — default needed) AND price breaks above the cluster → long. Target: SMA200 on the entry timeframe if price is below it, else the measured move.
13. **Multi-timeframe oversold:** SR(15m) OS AND SR(1h) OS (ideally also SR(4h) OS) at support → long. Hold through the 1h, then the 4h, if they keep rising.
14. **Divergence trick:** bullish (or hidden bullish) RSI divergence on 1h/4h → wait for a higher low → long on the neckline break of the inverse H&S. Target SMA200.
15. **Pattern breakouts:** falling wedge / cup-and-handle / double bottom / bull flag → enter on a candle close above the pattern line or rim. Target = measured move (cup depth, flagpole length, base height). Stop below the handle or last swing low.
16. **7m counter-move:** all higher timeframes OB but SR(7m) OS → small long scalp. Exit when SR(7m) is OB.

**D. Short entries**
17. **Failed-high short (his named strategy):** on the 4h (or 15m for scalps), SR rises back to OB AND price fails to exceed the previous swing high (or is rejected at resistance) → short. Stop above the previous high. Best when the 4h has made a lower high and the daily is bearish.
18. **Bear consolidation:** bear flag or rectangle on the 15m/1h in a downtrend → short the breakdown. Target = measured move. Mid-scalp target = SMA21.
19. **Insurance short:** SR(15m, 1h, 4h) all OB at resistance → small hedge short with a stop above resistance.
20. **SMA200 loss:** a 1h close below SMA200 after a bearish retest of it → short. Target SMA200(4h).

**E. Exits and management**
21. Mean reversion: IF price is extended far from SMA21 (1h or 4h) with SR OB THEN take profit. Expect a return to SMA21.
22. Long from below heading into SMA200 → sell 75% at SMA200 and move the stop to breakeven. A close above SMA200 → hold or add to the runner.
23. Move the stop to breakeven once price has covered about 30% of the distance to the target.
24. Take profit at resistance. On parabolic spot moves, recover the initial investment and keep a "moon bag."
25. Exit if the trade loses the trendline or pattern it was based on, or if a 1m/5m close breaks below SMA200 (on short-timeframe scalps).

**F. Portfolio structure**
26. Run two books: (a) a swing position from daily/4h levels with a stop under the daily SMA200 or a structural low, left alone; (b) 1h-and-lower scalps.
27. Keep a stablecoin reserve and never go all-in. Accumulate spot BTC when the 2-week/weekly SR is OS ("buy the red days").
28. Risk per scalp: small fixed dollars ($50–$485 in examples, typically about $75). Size down in volatile markets. Leveraged-token holds ≤ 12h.

## Gaps (a system builder must choose defaults)

- **Indicator settings:** Stoch RSI, RSI and MACD parameters are never stated (assume 14/14/3/3, 14, 12/26/9). No numeric OB/OS thresholds. The "dotted line" suggests 20/80 bands.
- **"Tight moving averages":** no numeric definition of compression, or of which MAs (21/50/100/200) must converge.
- **"Far from the 21":** no distance threshold (% or ATR) for mean-reversion exits.
- **Close confirmation:** one close or "multiple"? On which timeframe for which rule?
- **Pattern detection:** wedges, cups ("slanted", "shallow"), H&S, Bart Simpson and diamonds are drawn by eye. His 68% breakout odds are asserted, not sourced. He tolerates heavy subjectivity.
- **Stop placement:** usually "below previous support", "next low key area" or "below the 200". There is no ATR buffer and no maximum stop distance.
- **Position sizing:** no % of equity rule. Only dollar-risk anecdotes, plus a 2:1 minimum R:R (examples up to about 4:1). Leverage ranges from none to 34x with no rule.
- **Partial exits:** only the 75%-at-SMA200 rule is explicit. Otherwise, how to scale out and trail is undefined.
- **Timeframe hierarchy:** he mixes 30s, 1m, 5m, 7m, 15m, 1h, 4h, 12h, daily, 3-day, 5-day, weekly and 2-week. There is no fixed entry/confirmation/trend triple. The most consistent set is 4h trend, 1h trigger and 15m/5m timing.
- **Order-flow inputs:** Bookmap liquidity walls and liquidation spikes drive many intraday targets, with no rule for turning them into numbers.
- **Conflict resolution:** when pattern and oscillator disagree, he picks the oscillators ("I hit the stochastics when the stochastics are low", Kb0ZHTIp4Cc @ 1:50:14). This is not consistently applied.
- **Session filters:** "false move Monday", CPI days and Sunday nights are mentioned with no defined window.
- **Performance evidence:** win/loss claims ("7 for 7 insurance shorts", "10 for 13", "zero losses in a week") are self-reported and unverified. No backtest is ever shown.
