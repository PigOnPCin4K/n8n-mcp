## 12. Top 10 takeaways for a systematic strategy
1. Core signal: **SMA21 > SMA200, price consolidating, and the 21 touching price**. Long at the touch, stop under the 200 (or the last swing low under it), and require at least 3R to the measured-move or liquidity target. The mirror image applies for shorts.
2. Add a **cascade filter**: the 1m/5m/15m 200s are reclaimed in sequence, and the 1h and 4h sit on the same side of their 200s.
3. Prefer **entries close to the 200**. Skip trades when the 21 is far from the 200 or price is far from the 21 (stretched). Use that stretch, plus RSI/StochRSI overbought and bearish divergence, as the exit/scale-out trigger.
4. HTF regime: **weekly close vs. weekly SMA200**, plus the 5-day and 3-day SMA200 and candle-body closes vs. prior bodies. Weight longs only above the weekly 200; below it, favour shorts and bear flags.
5. **Candle-body reversal entries** on 4h and daily (green-after-red / tweezer), stop beyond the wick, expect a low hit rate, and move to BE quickly. This is the most mechanical of his setups and should be tested.
6. **Pattern break-and-retest with measured-move targets**. Trade only after the retest, never in the middle of the range.
7. **Swing/macro trigger:** 2-week StochRSI below the oversold line plus 2-week RSI bullish divergence near the weekly/2-week 200 for spot accumulation or wide-stop swings. It worked in 2026, but this is a single sample.
8. Risk: liquidation beyond the stop. Risk a fraction of realised profit (house money), downsize 3x after a losing streak, stop after a loss on a green day, and keep leverage at or below his 20–33x (a lower level, with fixed percent risk, is recommended).
9. **Time filters to test:** avoid weekends, Mondays before evening, Thursday after midday, and FOMC/CPI windows. Widen stops on low-liquidity weekends. Use the CME Sunday open as a volatility event.
10. Treat Bookmap, Fear & Greed, USDT dominance and "bottom" indicators as **discretionary context only**. His calls in this slice were roughly half right, so backtest A, C and D independently before trusting his anecdotes.
