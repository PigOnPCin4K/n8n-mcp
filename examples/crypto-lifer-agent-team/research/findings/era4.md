## 12. Top 10 takeaways for a systematic strategy
1. **Regime filter = 200 SMA ladder.** Long-only bias while BTC is above the 200 SMA on the 3D/weekly (and 5D). Treat a 4h close below its 200 SMA as the first warning, and the daily, 3D and weekly 200 SMAs as the escalation steps.
2. **Intraday direction** = which side of the 15m/1h/4h 200 SMA price closes on. Lose it and target the next higher-timeframe 200 SMA.
3. **Entry = break-and-retest** of a horizontal level, pattern line or POC, confirmed on the 1-7m charts. Skip the first breakout candle.
4. **Timing = stochastic RSI alignment.** The trigger timeframe and the next one up must be oversold (longs) or overbought (shorts). Veto the trade if the 15m disagrees with the 1h, or the 4h with the 1h.
5. **Core long pattern:** pullback into a flag that touches the 21 SMA, with low stochastics, the MACD histogram turning, and falling volume.
6. **Mean reversion:** price stretched from the 21 SMA with a divergence tends to snap back to the 21 and on to the 200 SMA. Testable on 1m-1h data.
7. **Volume-profile targets:** above the POC, expect travel through VRVP gaps to the next HVN or value-area high. Losing the POC targets the HVN or value-area low.
8. **Risk:** fixed 1% of equity per trade, a minimum 2.5-3R to the first target, liquidation beyond the stop, a stop beyond the 200 SMA or swing low, break-even after the first push, and take 50-75% off at the first resistance or 200 SMA.
9. **Filters:** avoid Sunday night to Monday morning and FOMC minutes. Context only: DXY trend, BTC.D and USDT.D both rising = risk-off.
10. **Leave out:** high leverage, averaging down, pattern "odds" he quotes without data, and Bookmap claims. Backtest every rule before trusting his stated percentages.
