## 2. Entry setups / triggers

**A. "Low stochs at bottom of pattern" long (core system)**
- **Timeframes:** 15m + 1h for entry, with the 4h/daily as filter. The 7m is used for fine-tuning.
- **Conditions:**
  - price is inside a bullish pattern (pennant, falling wedge, bull flag, bullish rectangle, channel) near its lower boundary
  - Stoch RSI is "below the dotted line" on the 15m and 1h
  - the 4h is not overbought
- **Direction:** long.
- **Process:** he projects the pattern hours ahead, sets an alert on the Stoch RSI, then enters "when the 15-minute stochastics are low in conjunction with the one-hour stochastics" [Mp6Oo7hPZBE @ 0:15:13].
- Other statements of the rule:
  - "My personal trading style is when the stochastics... are low in the one hour and the 15 minute I long" [NZlUlGDQXCE @ 0:53:07]
  - "this is my entry points low 15 minute low one hour" [KYiYeq4B8sc @ 1:58:58]
  - "I don't enter the trade... until price action comes down to the bottom of the pattern" [N0TTHSAPYn0 @ 0:20:32]
  - **Re-entry:** after taking profit, wait for price to flag back to the 21 MA while stochs reset, then enter again [Mp6Oo7hPZBE @ 1:41:01; ZGxteO-_6xU @ 0:20:21].

**B. Written checklist (daily/4h/1h BTC)** [UuqoAz4MjxQ @ 0:07:04–0:08:05]:
1. Price in a bullish pattern.
2. Price near the bottom of the pattern and in the last 25% of it.
3. MAs converging, with golden crosses (50/100 crossing the 200).
4. MACD turning bullish ("pink coming in, blue line curving").
5. RSI below 50, or in a falling wedge.
6. Stoch RSI below the dotted line.

He also requires divergence at the low: "my system would have had to find the divergence" [UuqoAz4MjxQ @ 0:05:03]. His usual multi-timeframe line is "four hours low, your long was right there on the one hour" [UuqoAz4MjxQ @ 0:07:04].

**C. Breakout-retest ("sniper retest")**
- **Timeframes:** 15m–4h.
- **Conditions:** price breaks a pattern boundary or resistance, then pulls back to the broken line (S/R flip) while the 15m stoch is low.
- **Direction:** long, or short for a bearish retest.
- Quote: "how did I know it was a retest because... the 15 minute is low" [o9eONKgsiBo @ 0:00:00] (BTC long at $54,300).
- "You don't have to chase green candles, you can always look for a re-test" [0EOYRJXR_-8 @ 0:47:06].

## 12. Top 10 takeaways for a systematic strategy
1. **Core entry:** long when price is in the lower part of a defined range/channel/pattern AND Stoch RSI is oversold on BOTH the 15m and 1h, with the 4h not overbought [Mp6Oo7hPZBE @ 0:15:13; KYiYeq4B8sc @ 1:58:58].
2. **Hard filter:** never open longs when the entry-timeframe Stoch RSI is above the upper band [nob6QJS-jxI @ 0:47:29].
3. **Exit rule:** take profit when the 1h Stoch RSI is overbought AND price is stretched from the 21-period MA; re-enter after price reverts to the 21 and stochs reset [Mp6Oo7hPZBE @ 1:41:01].
4. **Retest entries:** prefer retests over breakouts. After a level breaks, enter on the pullback to it with a low 15m Stoch RSI. For shorts, wait for the bearish retest, not the first breakdown [o9eONKgsiBo; hKqAyPAieDo @ 1:23:48].
5. **Divergence confirmation:** require bullish RSI divergence (regular or hidden) at the pattern low as an optional quality filter [ydTde2YROnI @ 0:42:15].
6. **Structural stops:** stops go below the last swing low, trend line, or golden pocket. Require at least 3:1 to the measured-move target capped at prior resistance. Take profit slightly before the target [rC6dgv3qPiY @ 1:35:52; jYFUD00pyjM @ 2:16:20].
