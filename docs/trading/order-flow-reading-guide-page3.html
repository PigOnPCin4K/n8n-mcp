5Lessons from this morning

The plan changed with the marketStarted in balance (mean reversion), switched to trend only after acceptance above VAH.
A loss is part of the systemTrade 2 followed every rule and still lost. Small loss, move on. One 3.5R winner covers three of them.
Skipping is a skillBoth ✗ setups had real buying, but no location. Not trading them saved money.
Stop when the edge is goneAfter the target, price sat in fat volume at lunch. No level, no aggression, so no more trades.
