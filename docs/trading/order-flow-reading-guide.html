How to Read Order Flow

An illustrated example of Fabio's Direction → Location → Aggression trend setup. Made-up data, drawn to teach.

1Price chart + volume profile

The profile (right) shows how much traded at each price. Fat = market agreed on price. Thin = price moved fast.

2CVD (cumulative volume delta), same timeline

Running total of aggressive buys minus aggressive sells. Up = buyers hitting the ask. Down = sellers hitting the bid.

3The 3 CVD patterns to recognize

Top line = price. Bottom line = CVD.

4The order book (DOM / SuperDOM ladder)

This is what you see in the NinjaTrader app. It shows orders waiting to trade, not trades that happened.

5Only trade when all three line up

DirectionOut of balance, and CVD agrees with the move.
LocationPrice is back at a thin spot (LVN) or the edge of value.
AggressionBig trades (bubbles) print in your direction right there.

Missing one? Stay flat. Risk 0.25% per trade, need 2:1 or better, stop after 3 losses. Educational illustration, not financial advice.
